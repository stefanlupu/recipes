### Ingredients:
* 225g unsalted butter, melted
* 250g granulated sugar
* 200g brown sugar, packed
* 4 large eggs, at room temperature
* 1 tablespoon vanilla | orange | peppermint extract
* 125g all-purpose flour
* 85g unsweetened cocoa powder
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* chocolate chips | chopped chocolate | walnuts (optional)
 
### Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Line a baking pan with parchment paper, leaving some overhang to make lifting the brownies out easier.
2. In a large bowl, combine the melted butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar. Stir well until smooth and glossy. Add the eggs, one at a time, mixing well after each addition. Stir in any extracts.
3. Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Gently fold everything together until just combined; avoid overmixing for best fudgy texture.
4. Add in the chocolate chips, chunks or nuts, if using, and fold gently to incorporate.
5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan, spreading it out evenly. Bake for about 30-45 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with a few moist crumbs. Do not overbake. 
6. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before lifting them out to cut into squares.
