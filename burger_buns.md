### Ingredients:
* 250g water
* 8g yeast
* 350g all purpose flour
* 100g strong bread flour
* 35g granulated sugar
* 10g fine salt
* sesame seeds
* 55g unsalted butter | vegetable oil

### Instructions:
1. Activate the yeast by heating the water (30-35°C) and mixing it in.
2. Combine all ingredients minus the fat of choice (oil | butter).
3. If kneading by hand add the vegetable oil before proceeding with the kneed.
4. If kneading with a machine start the process and slowly add the softened butter once the dough starts to come together.
5. Let the dough rise for 1h.
6. Divice dough into 6 equal pieces and shape them into round balls. Proof for 20 minutes on a baking tray ensuring they are covered with a damp towel or plastic wrap.
7. Preheat oven to 190°C. Egg wash the dough ball and generously sprinkle sesame seeds on top. Bake for 15-17m or until golden brown.
